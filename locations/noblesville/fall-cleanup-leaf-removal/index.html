Doing It Yourself?

Noblesville Leaf Pickup in 2026

The city runs both bagged and loose leaf pickup from October 12 through December 11, 2026. Bagged leaves have to go in the free compostable bags the city provides at fire stations, City Hall, the Parks Department, and the Street Department. Bags go between the street and the sidewalk on trash day, 40 pounds max, and there's no alley pickup.

For loose leaves, rake them to the edge of the street, not into it, and keep them separate from brush. The vacuum trucks run a continuous loop that takes about four weeks to cover the city, so a pile can sit at the curb a while. After December 11 the city asks residents to bag.

Burning isn't an option. Noblesville's open burning ordinance bans burning leaves and yard waste anywhere in city limits.

If You Do It Yourself

Noblesville Leaf Pickup Tips

  • Pick up the free compostable bags early. Only city bags are accepted for bagged pickup.
  • If you rake to the curb, keep the pile off the street and away from brush, or the vacuum crew can skip it.
  • The vacuum loop takes about four weeks to cover the city, so wet piles can sit and mat down the grass along the street.
  • After December 11, bag whatever is left.

Or Let Us Handle It

We clear leaves from the lawn and the beds. Everything leaves with us the same day. Gutter cleaning is available on request. Purdue's turf team says to remove leaves once you can't see the grass under them, so heavily wooded lots do better with recurring visits from late October into December.
